##e-learning.lagh-univ.dz_autologin_chrome-extension


After adding the extension to your chrome browser , click the icon and enter your credentials .
Disclaimers : 
- The extension never collects user data.
- your credentials are stored locally and in plain text (yes I know that I should encrypt/hash it but who cares about moodle accounts being stolen from your local browser, there is bigger problems to worry about if that happened).
